Test Goals:

The bike was tested for three main goals:

 - Safety and durability
 
 - 25 mph top speed
 
 - 30-mile range

Safety and Durability:

The bike remained mechanically secure throughout testing. The tires, wheels, axle nuts, torque arm, brakes, battery, controller, drivetrain, cables, and handlebars were inspected before riding. No exposed conductors or loose components were found.

During testing, the motor started and stopped correctly, both brake cutoffs worked, and the bike remained stable up to 25 mph with no wheel wobble, frame vibration, or component movement.

Braking was good from 5–20 mph and acceptable at 25 mph, although disk brakes would provide a shorter stopping time.

The battery measurements were consistent with a 13S lithium-ion pack. Voltage sag was only 0.2 V during flat-ground acceleration and 0.8 V during a hard hill climb.

The main concern was slight motor vibration and strained noise during heavy hill climbing. Motor and controller temperatures should be monitored during future tests.

Goal status: Largely achieved, with continued monitoring in the future needed.

25 mph Top-Speed Goal:

The bike reached 25.1 mph using throttle and 24.4 mph using pedal assist.

The bike remained stable at these speeds with no wheel wobble or component movement.

Acceleration averaged approximately 0–10 mph in 3.23 seconds and 0–15 mph in 5.32 seconds.

Goal status: Achieved.

30-Mile Range Goal:

The range test used a starting voltage of 54.6 V, an ending voltage of 49.6 V, a displayed battery SOC of 67%, and a distance covered of 12.1 miles.

 - Terrain was continuously hilly.
   
 - Assist level was mostly 7–9 out of 9.
   
 - Minimal pedaling used.

These were demanding conditions, and the battery still had significant energy remaining after 12.1 miles.

However, battery voltage cannot be used to accurately predict remaining range because lithium-ion voltage does not decrease linearly with state of charge. Therefore, the 30-mile goal cannot yet be confirmed without a full range test or Wh/mile measurement. A full range test will be conducted in the future.

Goal status: Promising but not yet verified.
